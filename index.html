Profile

Who is Aman Kumar?

Full profile

Aman Kumar is a software developer who builds local-first, open-source tools. The rule behind the work is simple: the program runs on your machine, the data stays there, and it does one job well.

His public work is published on GitHub as AMANKUMAR7777. It includes SiftAI, a file organizer driven by a local language model; PDS, which encodes files into printable A4 pixel grids; and GLSL-Stress, a ray-marching WebGL stress test. Smaller utilities, such as the terminal monitor sys-mon and the apt shortcuts in fapt, are maintained alongside them.

He works in Python, JavaScript, TypeScript and C++, reaches for WebGL when a page should feel alive instead of flat, and has used Fedora as a daily driver for years. Most of what he knows came from breaking something on Linux and then reading the source of the tool he broke.
