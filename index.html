About

Local first, cloud never

I am Aman Kumar. I write software that runs on your machine, keeps your data there, and does one job well. SiftAI sorts a messy folder with a local model. PDS puts files on paper. sys-mon watches the machine without eating it.

Most of what I know came from breaking things on Fedora, then reading the source of the tools I broke. I work in Python, JavaScript and TypeScript, reach for C++ when something needs to be fast, and use WebGL when a page should feel alive instead of flat.
